FIND THEM BEFORE THEY FIND YOU

Enemy snipers hide in the dark windows of the city — watch for the faint lens sparkle, then scope in and take the shot.

Scoping raises you out of cover: stay up too long and they lock on. A yellow glint means a shot is coming — duck, fast.

Ducking after the glint is a gamble; the volley may still clip you through the sandbags. Your rifle is bolt-action: ~2s between shots, so make them count.
